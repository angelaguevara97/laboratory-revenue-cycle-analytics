# Data Dictionary

All records in this project are synthetic. The fields are designed to represent common revenue cycle concepts without reproducing a real laboratory system.

## Patients

`patient_id` is a synthetic patient identifier.

`birth_year` supports age related analysis without storing a full date of birth.

`sex` is a synthetic demographic value used only for dataset variety.

`state` represents a synthetic patient state.

## Providers

`provider_id` is a synthetic ordering provider identifier.

`specialty` represents the provider specialty.

`state` represents the provider state.

## Payers

`payer_id` is the internal synthetic payer identifier.

`payer_name` is a fictional payer name.

`payer_type` groups the payer into a broad simulated category.

`filing_limit_days` is a simulated timely filing limit. It is not a real payer policy.

## Accessions

An accession represents the laboratory service event that connects the patient, ordering provider, payer, service date, test, diagnosis information, and charge.

`accession_id` is the unique accession identifier.

`patient_id` links the accession to a patient.

`provider_id` links the accession to an ordering provider.

`payer_id` links the accession to a payer.

`service_date` represents the date of service.

`collection_date` represents specimen collection.

`test_code` contains a laboratory CPT or HCPCS concept used for educational simulation.

`diagnosis_code` contains an ICD 10 CM concept or an intentionally missing or malformed value.

`charge_amount` is the synthetic gross charge.

`accession_status` indicates whether the accession is ready for billing or requires review.

## Eligibility

`eligibility_id` identifies the eligibility check.

`accession_id` connects the check to an accession.

`coverage_status` represents active, inactive, or unverified coverage.

`subscriber_match` indicates whether simulated subscriber information matches.

`authorization_status` indicates whether authorization is present, missing, or not required in the simulation.

`checked_date` records when the synthetic check occurred.

## Claims

`claim_id` identifies a synthetic claim.

`accession_id` links the claim to the laboratory service.

`submission_date` represents claim submission.

`claim_type` distinguishes original claims from future corrected or replacement claim records.

`charge_amount` is the gross billed charge.

`allowed_amount` is the synthetic amount recognized under the simulated payer arrangement.

`expected_reimbursement` is the synthetic expected payment after modeled payer and patient responsibility effects.

`payment_amount` is the actual payment generated by the simulation.

`claim_status` records the current claim outcome.

`original_claim_id` can connect future corrected claims to the original claim.

## Denials

`denial_id` identifies a denial record.

`claim_id` links the denial to a claim.

`denial_category` provides an operational category for analysis.

`simulated_reason_code` is deliberately labeled as simulated. It is not a real CARC or RARC value.

`denial_date` represents the date of the denial event.

`denied_amount` is the expected reimbursement at risk.

`resolution_status` represents recovered, pending, or written off outcomes.

`resolution_date` records the resolution date when applicable.

`recovered_amount` is the amount recovered after follow up in the simulation.

## Work Queue

`work_item_id` identifies a billing work item.

`accession_id` links the work to an accession.

`exception_type` describes the issue requiring attention.

`priority_score` is a synthetic operational score using filing urgency, value, and estimated resolution probability.

`queue_status` represents open, pending, resolved, or escalated work.

`created_date` is the date the item entered the queue.

`follow_up_date` is the planned follow up date.

`resolution_probability` is a synthetic modeling input used for prioritization. It is not a prediction from a real payer or laboratory.

## Claim History

`event_id` identifies an event.

`claim_id` links the event to a claim.

`event_type` describes the event such as submission or denial.

`event_date` records when the event occurred.

`notes` provides a short synthetic audit trail description.
