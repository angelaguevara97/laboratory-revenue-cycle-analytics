# Public Healthcare References

This project uses public healthcare concepts as background. The operational rules, payer names, financial arrangements, filing limits, and simulated coverage combinations in the application are not taken from these sources and do not represent real payer policy.

## CMS Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule

CMS publishes payment information for clinical diagnostic laboratory tests under the Clinical Laboratory Fee Schedule.

https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/fee-schedules/clinical-laboratory-fee-schedule-clfs

The CMS fee schedule files also state that the presence of a code or payment amount does not by itself imply Medicare coverage.

https://www.cms.gov/medicare/payment/fee-schedules/clinical-laboratory-fee-schedule-clfs/files

## CMS Clinical Laboratory Information

CMS maintains a Clinical Labs Information Center with laboratory coverage, payment, enrollment, and claims processing resources.

https://www.cms.gov/medicare/enrollment-renewal/providers-suppliers/information-centers/clinical-labs

## Payment and Remittance Advice

CMS explains that electronic and paper remittance advice communicates claim adjudication and payment information. Standard adjustment information can include Group Codes, Claim Adjustment Reason Codes, and Remittance Advice Remark Codes.

https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coding-billing/electronic-billing/health-care-payment-remittance-advice

## How These References Are Used

The project uses these materials to understand the general environment in which laboratory billing and remittance workflows operate. It does not copy a real payer's medical necessity policy, reimbursement contract, authorization policy, or filing limit.
